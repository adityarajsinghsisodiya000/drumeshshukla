Ancient Science of Healing

Panchkarma — The Five Purifications

Ayurveda's most powerful detoxification and rejuvenation system — healing the body from its deepest root.

Vamana

Controlled therapeutic vomiting to expel Kapha toxins from the upper body.

Asthma, Bronchitis, Sinusitis, Skin Disorders, Obesity, Diabetes

Virechana

Medicated purgation to cleanse the liver, blood, and intestines of excess Pitta.

Skin Diseases, Liver Disorders, Acidity, Inflammation, Hormonal Disorders, Diabetes

Nasya Karma

Administration of medicated oils through the nasal passage for head and neck disorders.

Sinusitis, Allergic Rhinitis, Cervical Spondylosis, Migraine, Facial Paralysis

Kati Basti

Warm medicated oil pooled on the lower back in a dough dam.

Lower Back Pain, Sciatica, Slip Disc, Lumbar Spondylosis

Abhyanga

Full body synchronized massage using specific medicated oils.

Neurological Disorders, Paralysis, Joint Pain, Stress, Fatigue

Leech Therapy

Application of medicinal leeches to purify blood and treat skin, vein and joint disorders.

Varicose Veins, Skin Disorders, Arthritis, Non-healing Wounds, Blood Disorders

Patra Pinda Sweda

Therapeutic fomentation using heated bundles of medicinal leaves.

Cervical Spondylosis, Lumbar Spondylosis, Arthritis, Joint Stiffness

Basti Karma

Medicated enema — the most potent treatment for neurological conditions.

All Vata Disorders, Sciatica, Paralysis, Spine Disorders, Constipation

Shirodhara

Continuous stream of warm medicated oil on the forehead.

Migraine, Insomnia, Anxiety, Depression, Stress, Memory Loss
