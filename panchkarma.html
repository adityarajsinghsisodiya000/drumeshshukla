Vamana

Therapeutic Emesis — Kapha Detox

Vamana is the first of the five classical Panchkarma procedures — a medically supervised therapeutic emesis designed to expel excess Kapha dosha from the stomach and respiratory tract. It is Ayurveda's most effective treatment for respiratory and skin disorders rooted in Kapha excess.
